# Audiobookshelf

Local Home Assistant add-on for Audiobookshelf.

## Open

After the add-on starts, open:

`http://HOME_ASSISTANT_IP:13378`

## Storage

- Config/database: `/data/config`
- Metadata/cache: `/data/metadata`
- Backups: `/data/metadata/backups`

Your Home Assistant media and share folders are mounted at `/media` and `/share`.
